Case Study 1: Helping Primary 4 Pupils Improve Reading Skills

Problem

Some Primary 4 pupils struggled to read simple words confidently. This affected their participation during lessons and made it difficult for them to keep up with classroom activities.

Action

I designed structured blending drills, introduced high-frequency sight words, and organised daily guided reading practice. I monitored each pupil's progress, adapted activities to meet individual learning needs, and encouraged consistent practice through positive support and feedback.

Result

The pupils gradually became more confident readers, improved their word recognition skills, and participated more actively during reading activities.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Communication
  • Problem Solving
  • Planning and Organisation
  • Patience and Empathy
  • Coaching and User Support

Case Study 2: Building My Professional Portfolio Website

Problem

As I transitioned into AI, IT Support, and Customer Support, I needed a professional online portfolio that would showcase my background, projects, certifications, and practical skills to potential employers.

Action

I planned the structure of the website and built it using HTML, CSS, GitHub, and GitHub Pages. Along the way, I learned how to create multiple web pages, connect navigation links, upload images, organise content, and troubleshoot issues such as missing pages, broken links, and image display problems. I also used AI as a learning partner to understand each step and improve the quality of my work.

Result

I successfully published a live portfolio website that highlights my professional background, case studies, resume, certifications, and contact information. The project strengthened both my technical confidence and my ability to solve problems independently.

  • HTML
  • CSS
  • ChatGPT

Skills Demonstrated
