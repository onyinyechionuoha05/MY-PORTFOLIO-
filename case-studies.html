Case Studies

These case studies highlight practical examples of how I solve problems, communicate clearly, and apply technology to support learning and improve everyday tasks.

Case Study 1: Helping Pupils Improve Reading Skills

Problem: Some Primary 4 pupils struggled with reading confidently and recognising common words.

Action: I created blending drills, introduced sight words, and organised daily reading practice to strengthen their reading skills through consistent practice.

Result: The pupils became more confident readers and showed noticeable improvement in their reading ability over time.

Case Study 2: Building My Portfolio Website

Problem: I needed a professional online portfolio to showcase my experience, projects, and skills.

Action: I built my own portfolio website using GitHub Pages, HTML, and CSS, creating multiple pages to present my background and work.

Tools Used: GitHub, GitHub Pages, HTML, CSS.

Result: I published a live portfolio website that demonstrates my projects and provides a professional online presence.

Case Study 3: Explaining Technical Concepts as an IT Support Trainee

Problem: Beginners often find technical instructions confusing and difficult to follow.

Action: I practised breaking technical concepts into simple, step-by-step explanations that non-technical users could easily understand.

Result: I strengthened my communication skills and my ability to support users by making technical information clear and practical.
