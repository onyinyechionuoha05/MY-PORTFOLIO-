Career Journey

My professional journey began in education, where I spent more than 15 years teaching, mentoring, and supporting learners. During this time, I developed strong communication, patience, organisation, and problem-solving skills that continue to shape the way I work today.

As technology became an important part of education and modern workplaces, I became interested in Artificial Intelligence and digital productivity tools. This inspired me to expand my skills into AI, IT Support, Customer Support, and Administrative Support.

Through the FlyRank AI Fluency Internship, I have completed practical projects involving AI-assisted writing, technical documentation, workflow improvement, portfolio development, and user-focused problem solving.

Today, I am building a career that combines my teaching experience with modern technology skills to support people, solve problems, and continue learning in an ever-changing digital world.
